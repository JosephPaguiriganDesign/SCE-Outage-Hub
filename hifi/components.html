A11y: one h2.hx-sg-h per group (tabindex=-1); each View/Hide details toggle has aria-expanded, aria-controls and the address in its name; focus stays on the toggle; Show fewer moves focus to the group heading; state is kept in the URL (?sg-open= lists the open toggles and is authoritative when present, written only when it differs from the defaults; ?sg-less=).
